ProductWORMWOOD OIL (Artemisia absinthium L.)
Product groupSpices & Condiments

Additional info

Origin: Artemisia absinthium (common wormwood) is a species of Artemisia, native to temperate regions of Eurasia and northern Africa. It grows naturally on uncultivated, arid ground, on rocky slopes, and at the edge of footpaths and fields.From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: Wormwood oil is an ingredient in the spirit absinthe, and is used for flavouring in some other spirits and wines, including bitters and vermouth. It is the traditional colour and flavour agent for green songpeyon (Korean rice cake) and an additional ingredient Moroccan mint tea. The plant has also many therapeutic uses and exhibits antimicrobial activity.
Production: The oil is mostly obtained by steamdistillation of the leaves and/or blossom of the plants.
Data (not) used: The composition of oils obtained by different extraction techniques at different growing stages are all taken in consideration. The composition of the oil is very depending on the origin and the growing stage of the plants.
