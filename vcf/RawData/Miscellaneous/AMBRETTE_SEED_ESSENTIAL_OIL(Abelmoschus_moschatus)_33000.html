ProductAMBRETTE SEED ESSENTIAL OIL(Abelmoschus moschatus)
Product groupMiscellaneous

Additional info

Ambrette seed, Abelmoschus moschatus, originates from the hibiscus flower. It has an aromatic character, suitable for perfumes and essential oils, which resembles the fragrance of musk with sweet and flowery tones (Wkipedia).
