Additional info

Kombucha is a fermented beverage which is produced by fermentation of sugared tea using a symbiotic culture of bacteria and yeast, referred to as SCOBY (Wikipedia, 2024). This specific kombucha was made using a herbal infusion of Honeysuckle (Lonerica japonica) instead of tea.
