ProductORANGE OIL (Citrus sinensis L. Osbeck)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Sweet orange (Citrus sinensis) is the most commonly grown tree fruit in the world. Sweet orange accounts for 70% of citrus production. It grows in tropical and sub tropical areas, mainly in Brazil and Florida, USA. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: Orange oil is used as a flavouring in many food and drink products and for its fragrance in perfume and aromatherapy.
Production: Citrus oils are often obtained by cold pressing of the peel, but also of the whole fruit. These oils are more stable on storage than essence oils (obtained by extraction or distillation) because of the presence of non-volatile constituents which have antioxidative properties.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Different varieties such as blond, blood and navel are included and are mentioned in the references together with the origin as much as possible. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration
