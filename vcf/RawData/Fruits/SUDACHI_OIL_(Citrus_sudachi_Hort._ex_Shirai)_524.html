ProductSUDACHI OIL (Citrus sudachi Hort. ex Shirai)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Sudachi (Citrus sudachi Hort. Ex Shirai) is a small, round, green fruit. It is smaller (25-30 g) than the related Kabosu fruit. It is a sour citrus fruit and thought to be an ichang papeda-mandarin orange hybrid. The fruit is the symbol of the prefecture of Tokushima, Japan, where the majority of the fruit is cultivated. The plant has white flowers, which bloom in May and June; fruit harvesting starts in the fall. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: A slice of this fruit is served with many traditional Japanese dishes and some alcoholic beverages. It is used alongside two other citrus fruits, yuzu and kabosu. About half of the harvest is used for juice production. The flavour is used in ice cream, vodka coolers, ice pops and soft drinks.
Production: Citrus oils are often obtained by cold pressing of the peel. These oils are more stable on storage than essence oils (obtained by extraction or distillation) because of the presence of non-volatile constituents which have antioxidative properties.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration
