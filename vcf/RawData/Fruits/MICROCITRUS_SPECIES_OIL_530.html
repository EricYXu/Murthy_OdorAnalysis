ProductMICROCITRUS SPECIES OIL
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Lime is a term referring to a number of different citrus fruits. This product MICROCITRUS contains data from “Australian native limes”. The varieties are known as: Microcitrus australasica (finger lime, see picture), M. australis (round lime), M. garrawayi (mount white lime), M. inodora (Russell river lime), M. warburgania, M. papuana and Erocitrus glaucia (desert lime). From Home Citrus Growers
Use:
Production: Lime oils are obtained by cold pressing the fruit and/or peel followed by separation in a water and oil fraction and by distillation.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil.
