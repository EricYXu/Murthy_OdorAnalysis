ProductGRAPEFRUIT JUICE (Citrus paradisi)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Grapefruit juice is obtained by squeezing grapefruits (Citrus paradisi).
Use: The juices are rich in Vitamin C and range from sweet-tart to very sour..
Production: Depending on the squeezing pressure, more or less volatiles from the peel are introduced in the juice. Fresh juice is on the market as pasteurized juice which should be stored refrigerated. Most juice is sold as reconstituted product prepared from desiccated juice. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Data (not) used: Differences in varieties of grapefruits are not taken in consideration. As good as possible only the data obtained from freshly produced juice are used. Volatile composition (flavour) changes due to packaging and subsequent storage and due to new processing techniques are not taken into consideration. All concentrations are based on single strength juice.
