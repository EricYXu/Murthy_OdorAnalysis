ProductMANDARIN OIL (Citrus nobilis)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: large citrus tree having large sweet deep orange fruit that is easily peeled; widely cultivated in Florida (syn: temple/king orange) It seems to be the consensus that the King group originated in Indo-China, whence it spread northward as far as Japan and southward throughout Malaya. Many of the mandarins currently found in the Malayan region and a few in China appear to be of the nobilis type. Some of the characters exhibited by this group are orange-like and others are intermediate between that fruit and mandarin, which supports the conclusion that the King type probably originated as a hybrid between the two and is therefore a natural tangor. From citruspages
Use: .
Production: Citrus oils are often obtained by cold pressing or distillation of the peel, but also of the whole fruit.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration
