Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: The grapefruit (Citrus paradisi), is a subtropical citrus tree known for its bitter fruit, an 18th-century hybrid first bred in Barbados. These evergreen trees are usually found at around 5–6 meters (16–20 ft) tall, although they can reach 13–15 meters (43–49 ft). The fruit is yellow-orange skinned and largely an oblate spheroid; it ranges in diameter from 10–15 cm. The flesh is segmented and acidic, varying in color depending on the cultivars, which include white, pink and red pulps of varying sweetness.From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: The family of flavours range from highly acidic and somewhat bitter to sweet and tart.
Production: Grapefruit oils are obtained by cold pressing followed by separation in a water and oil fraction and by distillation. Both types of oil are compiled together.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration.
