Product category SAKE (non-categorised)
Product groupAlcoholic Beverages < 20% alc.

Additional info

Sokujo-brewed sake is a traditional Japanese alcoholic beverage in which lactic acid is directly added to the brewing process. This results in a faster fermentation process compared to the Yamahai-brewed sake.
