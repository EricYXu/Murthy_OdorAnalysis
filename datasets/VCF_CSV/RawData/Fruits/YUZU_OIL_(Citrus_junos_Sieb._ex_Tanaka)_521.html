ProductYUZU OIL (Citrus junos Sieb. ex Tanaka)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Yuzu is a hybrid of Ichang papeda and sour mandarin (Citrus ichangensis × C. reticulata, formerly C. junos Siebold ex. Tanaka). Yuzu fruits, which are very aromatic, typically range between 5.5 and 7.5 cm in diameter, but can be as large as a grapefruit (up to 10 cm or larger). The Yuzu originated in China, and also grows wild in central China and Tibet. It was introduced to Japan and Korea during the Tang Dynasty and it is in these nations that it is cultivated most widely. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: In Japanese cuisine it is rarely eaten as a fruit, its outer rind is used to garnish dishes, its juice is commonly used as a seasoning, somewhat like lemon. It is also used in sauces and sweets. In Korean cuisine it is used, thinly sliced to make a thick, marmalade-like syrup containing pieces of the chopped rind and fruit. It is also used to make a traditional fruit punch.
Production:
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Differences between Japanese and Korean Yuzu and changes in volatile composition due to storage are not taken into consideration
