Additional info

Origin: Mandarins (Citrus reticulata) were grown in China and Japan on a large scale since the 16th century. Mandarin was the last of the important citrus fruits to arrive in Europe as late as the early 19th century. Commercial production of mandarins started in the early 1900's. The biggest mandarin producers today are China, Spain, Japan, Brazil and Turkey. The biggest mandarin exporters are Spain, China, Morocco, Turkey and South Africa. Mandarin, its hybrids and relatives together form the largest group in the genus Citrus. The number of commercially important mandarin varieties almost equals the number of all other horticultural Citrus types combined. From Citrus pages
The classification of mandarins is rather complicated. We have classified clementines under Mandarin (Citrus reticulata). Use: Only a small amount of mandarin juice is produced.
Production: Depending on the squeezing pressure, more or less volatiles from the peel are introduced in the juice.
Data (not) used: Only the data obtained from freshly produced juice are used. Volatile composition (flavour) changes due to packaging and subsequent storage are not taken into consideration. All concentrations are based on single strength juice.
