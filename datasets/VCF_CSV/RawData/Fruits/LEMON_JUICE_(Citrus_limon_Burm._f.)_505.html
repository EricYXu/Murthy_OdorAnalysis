ProductLEMON JUICE (Citrus limon Burm. f.)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Lemon (Citrus limon) is the ellipsoidal yellow fruit from an evergreen tree native to Asia. Now it grows in tropical and sub tropical areas.
Use: Lemon juice is used in a wide variety of food and drink to make lemonade, soft drinks, and cocktails. It is used in marinades for fish, where its acid neutralizes amines in fish by converting them into non-volatile ammonium salts, and meat, where the acid partially hydrolyzes tough collagen fibers, tenderizing the meat, but the low pH denatures the proteins, causing them to dry out when cooked. Lemon juice is also used as a short-term preservative on certain foods that tend to oxidize and turn brown after being sliced. Lemon juice is used to make marmalade and lemon liqueur.
Production: Depending on the squeezing pressure, more or less volatiles from the peel are introduced in the juice.
Data (not) used: As good as possible only the data obtained from freshly produced juice are used. Volatile composition (flavour) changes due to packaging and subsequent storage and due to new processing techniques are not taken into consideration. All concentrations are based on single strength juice.
