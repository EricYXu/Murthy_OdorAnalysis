ProductKINOKUNI MANDARIN JUICE (Citrus kinokuni)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: The kinokuni mandarin is said to be a very old species of Chinese origin and one of the earliest introduced into Japan, where it is still popular because of its pleasant flavour and rich fragrances. At least four varieties are known, the largest of which is Hirakishu. From citruspages
Use: The fruit is small, somewhat oblate, depressed at both ends, orange-coloured, and glossy. The thin, comparatively tight rind is easily peelable and fragrantly aromatic. The flesh is firm, meaty, and pleasantly sweet in flavour. Seeds are few.
Production: Depending on the squeezing pressure, more or less volatiles from the peel are introduced in the juice.
Data (not) used: Differences in varieties of Kinokuni mandarinfruits are not taken in consideration. Only the data obtained from freshly produced juice are used. Volatile composition (flavour) changes due to packaging and subsequent storage are not taken into consideration. All concentrations are based on single strength juice.
