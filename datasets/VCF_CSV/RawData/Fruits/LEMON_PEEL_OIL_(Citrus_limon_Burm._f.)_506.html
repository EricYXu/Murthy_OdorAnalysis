ProductLEMON PEEL OIL (Citrus limon Burm. f.)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Lemon (Citrus limon) is the ellipsoidal yellow fruit from an evergreen tree native to Asia. Now it grows in tropical and sub tropical areas.
Use: Lemon oil is used as a flavouring in many food and drink products and for its fragrance in perfume and aromatherapy.
Production: Lemon oils are obtained by cold pressing of the peel, or by distillation of the leaves. In this product only the peel oils are compiled.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration
