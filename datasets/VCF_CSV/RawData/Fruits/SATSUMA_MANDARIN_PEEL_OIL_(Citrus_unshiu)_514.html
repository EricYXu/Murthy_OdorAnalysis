ProductSATSUMA MANDARIN PEEL OIL (Citrus unshiu)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: This mandarin is the famous and highly important Unshû mikan (Unshiu) of Japan. Satsuma is the name of a former province, now Kagoshima Prefecture, on the southern tip of Kyushu Island, where it is believed to have originated.From Citrus pages
Use: Fruit are medium-small to medium, oblate to subglobose and seedless. Orange-coloured but commonly matures prior to development of good colour. Rind thin, somewhat leathery; surface moderately smooth and with large and prominent oil glands; easily separable. Segments 10 to 12, with tough carpellary membranes, loosely separable; axis hollow. Flesh orange-coloured; tender and melting; flavour rich but subacid.
Production: Mandarin oils are obtained by cold pressing followed by separation in a water and oil fraction and by distillation. Both types of oil are compiled together.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration.
