ProductLIME OIL (coldpressed)
Product category CITRUS FRUITS
Product groupFruits

Additional info

Origin: Lime is a term referring to a number of different citrus fruits, both species and hybrids, which are typically round, green to yellow in color, 3–6 cm in diameter, and mostly containing sour and acidic pulp. In this product only data from Citrus aurantiifolia, also called Mexican lime, and Citrus latifolia, also called Persian lime, are used. "Australian lime" is treated as separate product under MICROCITRUS species. Limes are grown worldwide all year round and are usually smaller and sweeter than lemons. From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Use: Lime oil is used as a flavouring in many food and drink products and for its fragrance in perfumes, cleaning products and aromatherapy.
Production: Lime oils are obtained by cold pressing followed by separation in a water and oil fraction and by distillation. In this database both types of oil are considered as separate products.
Data (not) used: Almost all literature gives the volatile composition as percentage of the essential oil. Changes in volatile composition due to ripening and storage are not taken into consideration.
